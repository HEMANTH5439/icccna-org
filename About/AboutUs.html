About icccna

Our Children and Youth Christian Basketball Tournament was founded by Dr. Regi Paul in year 2001 on Northwest side of City of Chicago as an extension of Passion Play of Christ “ Our Children, Our Good Friday” Ministry. The Primary purpose of Indo-American Christian Children of Chicago Basketball Tournament is to promote friendships and develop Basketball skills among the Christian youths and their friends in City of Chicago and its Suburbs.

Indo-American Christian Youth Basketball Tournament over the years has dramatically improved Basketball skills and have now poised to form "First National Christian Youth Basketball Team" to compete with other States in America.

  • Passion Play of Christ

    "Our Children, Our Good Friday” is a Community Passion Play of Christ was started by Dr. Regi Paul in 1998. The program is designed to teach children power of peace and non-violent techniques based on Jesus Christ's "Seven Words on the Cross" and its application for peace and harmony among all the nations of world. We strive to “Awaken the Child's faith in oneself by uniting the communities together with the hearts and hands of our children. We explore critical thinking skills to help our children understand the world around them by using the life choices of Christ as a teaching tool

  • Bible Spelling Bee Championship

    Our mission and purpose of the Bible Spelling Bee for India Christian Children of Chicago and North America is based on Revelations 1:3, “Blessed is he who reads aloud the words of the prophecy, and blessed are those hear, and who keep what is written therein; for the time is near.” Based on this passage, children are to read, learn, and make part of themselves the words of Bible, and believe in the prophecy. The Bible is the basis of our life and existence. The Bible is not only the best book to learn, but also the only book to live according to the Will of God. Spelling Biblical words every day, a child can discover core purposes and meanings of their life on this earth.

  • Christian Youth Basketball Tournament

    Indo-American Christian Children and Youth Basketball Tournament was founded and sponsored by Dr. Regi Paul and Mrs. Rita Paul to build the Youth Basketball Ministry in our Lord. The primary purpose of our Basketball Tournament is to encourage physical activities and reduce juvenile diabetes among our children and youth of Christian Community of Chicago. The highlight of our Youth Basketball Tournament is that the Scripture Bible verse is displayed on player uniforms to evangelize the Word of God as commanded in Matthew Gospel 28:19. Our goal is to bring together all our community youth as one functional unit in the Body of Christ.

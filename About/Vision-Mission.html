Vision & Mission

We strive to “Awaken the Child's faith in oneself by uniting the communities together with the hearts and hands of our children. We explore critical thinking skills to help our children understand the world around them by using the life choices of Christ as a teaching tool. We help prepare them to understand value of Power of Peace and to contemplate on many hard decisions encountered in life of Christ. Christ could have denied his responsibility , run or even struck out at his enemies, yet his belief in Himself and compassion for others and obedience to the will of God drove him to do what he knew was right. This is the strength and resolve we hope to awaken in child for a better world of tomorrow. We teach Children Peace not War.

Our mission and purpose of the Bible Spelling Bee for India Christian Children of Chicago and North America is based on Revelations 1:3, “Blessed is he who reads aloud the words of the prophecy, and blessed are those hear, and who keep what is written therein; for the time is near.” Based on this passage, children are to read, learn, and make part of themselves the words of Bible, and believe in the prophecy. The Bible is the basis of our life and existence. The Bible is not only the best book to learn, but also the only book to live according to the Will of God. Spelling Biblical words every day, a child can discover core purposes and meanings of their life on this earth.

Our Mission is to promote friendship, unity, harmony and to develop spirit of sportsmanship among all children and youth of our Christian Community through our four ministries: Passion Play of Christ, Bible Spelling Bee Championship, Basketball Tournaments and Tithing and Financial Conferences. Our primary goal is to motivate and increase physical activities and reduce obesity and prevent juvenile diabetes among the children and youth of our Community.
